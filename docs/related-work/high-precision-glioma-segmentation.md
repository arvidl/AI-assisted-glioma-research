## What the study contributes

The unified MONAI BraTS three-compartment SegResNet pipeline uses T1, T1c,
T2 and FLAIR. Tumor segmentation is combined with subject-specific FreeSurfer
anatomical parcellation in the reviewed subject coordinate space. Compartment-region intersections produce physical
volumes and regional tumor-burden hit-plots. The published evaluation uses
50 UCSF-PDGM glioblastoma subjects (IDH-wildtype, WHO grade 4). Reported median
Dice is 0.90 for whole tumor, 0.94 for tumor core and 0.86 for enhancing tumor;
corresponding median HD95 is 4.1, 2.2 and 2.0 mm. A single LUMIERE subject
illustrates six longitudinal examinations. These are **published reported
results**, not new measurements or synthetic-example performance.

The checked aggregate record is src/glioma_ai/references/lundervold2026.json.
The package exports a separate published_imaging_reference.json and
published_numbers.tex. Synthetic metrics.json remains a record of generated
teaching constructions. No image model is trained or executed for this citation.

## Bridge to biology and clinical questions

Regional burden supplies descriptive anatomical context for where to study
a glioma–brain interaction. It does not establish functional eloquence,
neuron–glioma synapses, malignant-cell programs, prognostic value or clinical
utility. The published limitations explicitly exclude patient-outcome and
clinical-utility evaluation and call for further validation.

A possible next study would connect reviewed regional profiles to appropriate
tissue assays, physiology or patient outcomes. Sampling, spatial correspondence,
treatment context, uncertainty, confounders and held-out evaluation would need
specification. MRI label volumes cannot silently become tumor density or
coefficients of the teaching models.

The talk devotes three early slides to methods, regional anatomy and reported
accuracy, with the hit-plot in backup. Notebook 09 gives the full bridge;
01 and 09 retain its measurement and modeling connections. Publication findings, biological
literature, synthetic models and proposed follow-up remain separately labeled.

`src/glioma_ai/references/imaging_figures.json` records source paths, SHA-256
hashes, author revision, publication locators, captions and CC BY attribution.
The pinned revision is a **local author-manuscript checkout**, not the cleaned
public code revision. The cleaned public snapshot does not contain these assets.
With an author checkout containing the revision:

~~~sh
make published-figures HPGS_REPO=/absolute/path/to/author-manuscript-checkout
make paper
make talk
~~~

The importer reads Git blobs at the pinned revision, verifies all three hashes
before writing, and preserves their bytes. It writes only ignored
`paper/generated/` files and a local import record. There is no network fallback,
data acquisition or trained inference. CI and source-ZIP builds without this
optional import retain notices and the reported numeric results. This difference
between the fully illustrated local PDFs and clean-source builds is intentional.
